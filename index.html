Our Story

Oyelekan Africa Limited is a premium unisex fashion brand based in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria. We specialize in bespoke wears that celebrate the African identity through a modern, sophisticated lens.

Every piece is meticulously crafted, blending traditional textures with contemporary silhouettes. Whether it's a tailored suit or an Ankara-inspired statement piece, our designs are made to empower and elevate.
